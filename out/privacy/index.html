Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joza Solutions LLC (“Joza Solutions”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with Caster Plus (the “App”), an Android app that mirrors your phone’s screen and sound to a TV you choose and plays videos you choose on that TV. It describes the information the App uses, where that information is sent, and what we retain.

Caster Plus does not collect personal information. The App has no accounts, analytics or advertising, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from it. Content you choose to mirror or send is transmitted directly from your phone to your TV.

1. Information the App uses

Information When it is used Where it is sent
Your screen (Android’s screen-sharing prompt) Only while you mirror, after you approve Android’s prompt Directly to the TV you selected, over your Wi-Fi network, encrypted from your phone to that TV
Sound that apps are playing (“Record audio” permission) Only while mirroring with “Send sound to the TV” turned on. The microphone is never used Directly to the TV you selected, encrypted
A video, audio file or link you share with the App Only when you use Send to TV To the TV you selected, over your Wi-Fi network. The file name is shown on the TV as its title. For a link, the TV downloads the video from that website
Your local network (“Nearby devices” permission) To find TVs and connect to them Stays on your network
Your phone’s model name and a random identifier created by the App When connecting to a TV To that TV, so it can show which device is connecting and remember the pairing
Notifications (optional) While casting, to show Stop and Pause controls Stays on your phone

The App cannot browse your photos or files; it can access only the item you share with it. It does not record, save or upload your screen, sound or videos.

Anyone who can see the TV can see your screen, including notifications that appear while you mirror. To share less, mirror a single app instead of the whole screen. Apps that protect their content, such as paid streaming services, appear black and silent on the TV; the App does not circumvent that protection.

2. Information stored on your phone

The App stores the pairing keys for TVs you have paired with (so you enter a TV’s code only once), your settings, and any TVs you added by address. This information is kept in the App’s private storage, is excluded from device backups, and is deleted when you uninstall the App. The App keeps no usage logs. Android’s system log on your phone may contain technical error messages from the App; these leave your phone only if you send them to us with Email support.

3. Information we receive

  • Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to respond to you, which is our legitimate interest in handling your request, and we delete them 12 months after your last message, or sooner if you ask. If you use Email support in the App, the draft includes the error, your phone’s model and Android version, the App’s version, and a diagnostic log from the App. The log can contain your TVs’ names and network addresses and the titles of videos you sent. You can remove it from the draft, and nothing is sent unless you send the email.
  • Purchase records. When you buy the App, Google Play provides us with an order number, the date and price, and the buyer’s general location (country, and sometimes state, city or postal code). We do not receive your payment card details. We keep these records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Because there is no tracking, Do Not Track signals have no effect on the App.

  • Google processes your purchase under its own privacy policy. It provides us with sales reports and, if your phone shares diagnostics with Google, anonymous crash reports.
  • Your TV and its manufacturer’s software handle what the TV displays under their own policies. Some TVs can analyze what is on screen; review your TV’s privacy settings.
  • Websites. When you send a link to the TV, the TV downloads the video from that website.

5. Security

  • Mirroring is encrypted from your phone to the TV. Before sending anything, the App verifies that the TV is the device it claims to be: an Apple TV or AirPlay TV through the pairing you confirmed with its on-screen code, and a Google Cast device through Google’s device certificates.
  • Send to TV runs a small file server on your phone only while a video plays. Only the TV you selected can connect, and the link is random and stops working when playback ends. The transfer itself is not encrypted on your Wi-Fi network, as with other media servers on home networks, so use Send to TV on networks you trust.
  • Pairing keys remain in the App’s private storage on your phone.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We designed the App so that as little information as possible leaves your phone.

6. Google Play data safety

Our Google Play listing states that Caster Plus collects no data and shares none. Google considers data collected when an app sends it to the developer or to a third party. Caster Plus sends your screen, sound and videos only to your own TV on your own network, and never to us or anyone else.

7. Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We hold no record of how you use the App, and we disclose support emails or purchase records only where the law requires it.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change would cause the App to send any new information off your phone, the App will notify you before it does so.
